How PLURA Works

A three-stage AI pipeline that mirrors the diagnostic reasoning of an expert radiologist.

1

Lung Segmentation

Stage 01

Isolates the left and right lung regions from the raw chest X-ray using a dedicated segmentation model. Background structures are removed, focusing all subsequent analysis exclusively on pulmonary tissue.

2

Symmetry Comparison

Stage 02

Uses Vision Transformers (ViT) to compare texture and density between the left and right lungs via self-attention. Asymmetric opacity patterns — a primary hallmark of pneumonia — are detected with high sensitivity across patch embeddings.

3

Context-Aware Analysis

Stage 03

Mimics radiologist reasoning by evaluating local abnormalities within a global thoracic context. Regional findings are interpreted relative to the full lung architecture, reducing false positives from anatomical variation.

Pipeline runs end-to-end in under 2 seconds on standard hardware.
